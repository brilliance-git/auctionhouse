# WoW: Forever — Protection Warrior Solo Instance Gold Farms (Research Handoff)

**Compiled:** 2026-11-23 (Forever live patch 1.60.1, servers launched 2026-11-04)
**Purpose:** Context brief for another Claude session. Everything below was gathered via web search on 2026-11-22/23. Confidence labels are included because Forever data is new and much of it is still Classic-carryover or beta data.

---

## 0. Ground rules / context the next session needs

- **Game:** World of Warcraft: Forever (Blizzard's "Classic+" with new dungeons, reworked Protection Warrior, new zones like Riverglades). NOT retail, NOT Classic Era/Anniversary, though Classic data is often reused.
- **Instance cap:** Blizzard hotfixed the limit from **10 → 5 new instances per hour** (~Nov 21, 2026), matching Classic Era. Target **≤12 min per run** to use all 5 resets. Wowhead notes full clears take 30+ min and won't hit the cap; farms under ~12 min will. (Source: Wowhead news 383950)
- **Prot warrior in Forever is a real solo AoE class**, unlike Classic. Rage is generated from blocks/dodges/parries; guides report holding **10–15 mobs rage-capped**. ConquestCapped calls it "best AoE farmer after the Paladin nerfs".
  - Farming talents: Shield Specialization 5/5, Master of Defense 2/2 (ConquestCapped). Icy Veins has a separate 2/17/32 "Dungeon Farming Build" focused on rage gen + Piercing Howl.
  - AoE priority (Wowhead level-30 prot guide): Charge → Bloodrage → Thunder Clap → Shield Block → Revenge → Demoralizing Shout → Rend on multiple targets → Cleave with excess rage.
  - Gear/race: health-regen gear; Undead (Touch of the Grave) is recommended for solo farming; Troll regen racials also good. Troll's Blood Elixir for chain pulling. Weak vs casters/elementals (Icy Veins).
- **⚠️ Exploit risk:** Blizzard confiscated gold and suspended "significant abusers" of the Blackmaw Hold farm (Nov 16–17, 2026). Blizzard said occasional normal runs were fine; deliberate repeated abuse was punished. **Avoid pathing/leash/ledge exploits.** Stick to normal pull-and-kill farms.
- **Not verified:** No source shows a prot warrior earning >20 g/hr on live servers with documented numbers. Everything above that is extrapolation.

## 2. Farm details + drop tables

### 2.1 Ruins of Lordaeron — trash loop (BEST DOCUMENTED WARRIOR FARM)

- **Where:** Portal above Undercity, left of the ruined courtyard gate (Tirisfal). Forever-only dungeon, tuned levels 15–20. Horde-oriented; the rare Lordaeron Captain is only attackable by Horde.
- **Route (ConquestCapped warrior section):** clear both rows of spiders → banshees and wraiths → gargoyles at the top → leave and reset. ~10 min/run.
- **Reported income (ConquestCapped):** beta 6–8 g vendor + 1–2 BoE blues at 6–7 g each = ~11–20 g/hr. Live: expect low end (blue prices dropped). Their table lists the warrior farm at level 30.
- **Trash notes (wowforever.be, wowforeverguides):**
  - Spiders: Venom Lurkers, Spiders, Tarantulas, Deep Widows, Broodlings (poison); some packs patrol.
  - Skeletal Soldiers + Shrieking Banshees near The Baron; banshees **silence** — stun the packs. Wailing Banshees apply a hit-reducing curse.
  - **Stone Watchers (gargoyles):** pose as statues; mid-fight turn to stone — **immune to physical and regenerate**. This is a real problem for a warrior; wait out the stone phase or skip if it slows you down.
- **Trash drops (ConquestCapped RoL guide):**
  - Rotmender's Raiment (healer cloth set): Rotmender's Gloves from Stone Watcher ~8%; Rotmender's Sash and Rotmender's Garb from trash (no rate).
  - Intact Limbs — from undead, often several per kill (quest: Light's Justice, 25 needed).
  - Bloodied Insignia — from undead, starts an Alliance quest.
  - Plus generic coin/grey vendor items (not itemized anywhere).
- **Rare: Lordaeron Captain** (roams NW on some runs): Haunting Blade (2H sword, healing), Phantom Armor (mail chest), both level 22.

### 2.2 Zul'Farrak — Graveyard (best g/hr candidate, NOT warrior-tested)

- **Where:** Tanaris. Forever lists ZF as level 44–54 (Mobalytics), recommended ~48.
- **Why it's a candidate:** Classic's staple AoE farm. Forever: solo streamers report **40–50 g/hr** (mage; ConquestCapped). YouTube: "WoW Forever: Solo ZF Farm – 50 Gold Per Hour" (mage).
- **Warrior fit (inference):** graveyard is mostly melee zombies; prot can hold 10–15. Mmonster notes Blizzard weakened mage slows in Forever — "test before planning gold on it". Classic warlocks did it in ~3 big pulls; mages in one.
- **Mob: Zul'Farrak Zombie** — level 43–44 elite undead, avg ~3s 48c coin per kill (wowclassicdb, Classic data). Spawned from graves near Witch Doctor Zum'rah.
- **Drops:**
  - Mageweave Cloth — **low rate** on zombies (Mmonster), but volume compensates. Older Classic guides claim 40–60 (or 50–100) Mageweave per full ZF/graveyard run; treat as Classic upper bound.
  - Grey vendor trash; random world-drop greens/blues (wowclassicdb zombie table shows world-drop items like Gloves of Holy Might, Ardent Custodian, Dazzling Longsword, Fiery War Axe, Robes of Insight, Blackskull Shield — no rates).
  - Classic estimate (noobtoboss, mage): ~10–15 g raw vendor per graveyard clear. Classic only.
- **Mage-only Forever numbers; no warrior g/hr exists.**

### 2.4 Scarlet Monastery — Graveyard / Library

- **Level:** 32–40 group; Cathedral listed as solo at 60 (wowcalc, mage).
- **Beta vendor-only rates (Mobalytics rogue guide, 892 chests):** GY ~7.5 g/hr, Library ~9 g/hr; with AH sales ~10 / ~12–14 g/hr. **But the solo chest route was hotfixed ~Oct 19** (epiccarry) — those rates came from a chest route that no longer works; treat as an upper bound.
- **Drops:** Silk Cloth (main), Mageweave ~5% (Warcraft Wiki), greens, vendor trash. Library has Fadeleaf herb spawns (Herbalism 160).
- Cathedral (wowcalc): Scarlet troops 34–40, ~2s 68c per kill, Silk + Mageweave + greens.

### 2.5 Krol'dok Stronghold (fast clears; famous version is a mage exploit)

- Wowhead: "a farm that can be completed in about 5 minutes". GameRant: >100 g/hr (109 g/hr, >8 g per pull) but via **mage standing on a high ledge casting Blizzard while mobs path in circles** = pathing exploit. Not warrior-doable, and risky given Blackmaw enforcement.

### 2.6 Dire Maul East — Whip Lashers (level 60 candidate)

- **Mobs:** Whip Lasher — level 54, **non-elite, melee-only** plants in the Conservatory, gathered in two large pulls between pillars (wowcalc, mage). Elemental type, ~1,900 HP (wowclassicdb TBC data).
- **Why it fits a warrior (inference):** non-elite melee-only = ideal for rage-from-blocks AoE tanking.
- **Drops:** **Heart of the Wild 27.27%** (Metaforge, VMaNGOS data, "unchanged from Classic"; wowcalc says ~a quarter of kills — consistent). Living Essence (no rate). Low-rate herbs (~1.9% each Silverleaf/Mageroyal/Peacebloom/Earthroot/Briarthorn — wowclassicdb). Herb nodes in same room (Herbalism bonus).
- **Classic history:** Blizzard forum post says the warlock lasher farm was "nerfed to worthlessness" in Classic; Forever status unconfirmed. wowcalc flags it "Classic data: not yet confirmed in Forever".

### 2.7 Stratholme Living — Scarlet side (level 60 candidate)

- **Mobs:** Crimson soldiers (Scarlet Bastion, the Hoard, halls to Crimson Throne), elites 57–60, humanoid. ~4s 68c per kill (wowcalc; wowclassicdb shows 4s 65c for a Scarlet mob).
- **Drops:**
  - Runecloth on ~1/3 of kills (wowcalc).
  - **Righteous Orb:** ~1 in 12 kills (wowcalc); Crimson Monk 8% (wowclassicdb, TBC data). Archivist Galford drops orbs much more often — "almost always" (Wowhead comments), "100%" (Almar's), "~50%+" (Blizzard forum). Classic runs averaged ~3 orbs/run. Orbs are a Crusader enchant reagent (2x per enchant, plus 4x Large Brilliant Shard) — Forever demand exists (lfcarry, updated Nov 17 2026).
  - Crimson Monk table also has rare BS plans (Radiant Boots/Leggings, Whitesoul Helm, Thorium Leggings ~0.02–0.04%), Major Mana Potion.
- **Classic precedent:** a TBC Classic warrior pulled 200+ mobs in Strat (YouTube) using force reactive discs, free action potions and Figurine of the Colossus — gear Forever doesn't have.
- Must chunk the Scarlet side into ≤12-min runs for the 5/hr cap.

### 2.8 DEAD: Blackmaw Hold Devotee room

- Level 55–60 dungeon in Azshara. Room of ~30 non-elite Blackmaw Devotees dropped 25–50 s each + greens, vendor items, Motes of Unstable Magic, Blackmaw Pouches; 100–160 g/hr.
- **Hotfixed Nov 16, 2026: Devotees drop nothing.** Nov 17: Blizzard removed gold from a couple hundred heavy farmers and suspended the worst. Old videos advertising it are stale.

## 3. Classic / Anniversary / TBC history (for triangulation)

- In Classic/Anniversary, warriors were the **worst** solo farmers. Icy Veins: warrior tanks are poor world farmers; best warrior gold was **Dire Maul East** but needed a partner (healer) and Mining/Herbalism. Blizzard forum: DME is "the warrior's farm" but not soloable.
- Classic class farms that map onto Forever: Mage ZF graveyard / DME lashers; Warlock/Hunter Maraudon (Princess Theradras, Blackstone Ring); Hunter DM North tribute; Paladin Strat Living / ZG.
- TBC Classic: warrior mega-pulls (Strat 200+, Figurine of the Colossus) — relied on TBC items.
- Season of Discovery: warrior rated C-tier for solo farming.
- **Takeaway:** Forever's rage/prot rework is what makes solo warrior farming viable; Classic "warrior farm" guides mostly don't apply. Look for dense, melee-only, non-caster packs.
