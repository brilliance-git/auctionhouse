# Wowhead (WoW: Forever) drop tables for Classic dungeons, collected 2026-11-25

Source: the "Drops" tab of NPC pages on wowhead.com/forever. Percentages are Wowhead's drop count divided by the number of kills it recorded.

Which mobs are included: every mob in each dungeon that drops at least one blue, with at least 20 recorded kills. Mobs are sorted by blues per kill, highest first.

How to read an entry:
- "(low blue rate)" means fewer than 0.5 blues per kill. These are mostly trash, but Spawn of Hakkar, Marduk Blackpool and Vectus are named mobs that just drop few blues.
- Each entry lists up to three blues. "…" means the mob has more blue drops.
- "greens N%" is the expected number of green items per kill. It can be above 100%, meaning more than one green per kill on average.
- Wowhead doesn't record whether an item is bind-on-pickup or bind-on-equip. Look the item ID up on the Horde AH: an item that has ever been listed there is bind-on-equip.

What's covered:
- Fully collected: Sunken Temple, Blackrock Spire (LBRS), Blackrock Depths and Scholomance.
- Stratholme: 21 of 22 mobs collected.
- Not collected: Dire Maul, Maraudon, Uldaman, Zul'Farrak, Razorfen Downs, Scarlet Monastery, Razorfen Kraul, City of Dalaran and Ruins of Lordaeron. Wowhead's human-verification check stopped the collection again.
